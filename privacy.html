What this site collects

This is my personal portfolio. I use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use it, so I can improve it. Clarity records:

  • usage statistics, such as pages viewed, device, browser and approximate location
  • heatmaps of where people click and how far they scroll
  • session recordings of clicks, scrolls and mouse movement

Clarity masks sensitive content by default. I don't use this data to identify you.

Microsoft’s role

Microsoft collects this data through cookies and similar technologies, and also uses it as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. By using this site, you agree that I and Microsoft can collect and use this data.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For visits from these regions, Clarity doesn’t set cookies unless you give consent. This site doesn’t ask for consent, so Clarity runs without cookies for you.

Opting out

Clarity honours the Global Privacy Control signal, which some browsers and extensions can send for you. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
